## 4. Attack Vectors for a Per-Trainer, One-Time-Price Product

1. **Kill the per-client tax.** Both competitors charge by client-count buckets — a coach growing from 50 to 100 clients pays Trainerize roughly 2x and Everfit ~$45/mo more, forever. "Pay once per trainer, unlimited clients" directly weaponizes their core pricing model.
2. **Bundle what they unbundle.** Nutrition ($20–45/mo TZ, $39/mo Everfit), payments ($10/$9/mo), automation ($25/$29+/mo), on-demand content ($25/mo Everfit) are all paid add-ons. An everything-included one-time price converts the most cited complaint on both sides ("nickel and dime", "features locked behind add-ons").
3. **Subscription fatigue + ABC price-increase backlash.** Trainers are publicly shopping for exits over Trainerize price hikes (Facebook trainer groups, Gymkee). A "never pay a monthly fee again" message lands at the exact moment of churn intent.
4. **Branding without the Enterprise gate.** True white-label is $248+/mo (Trainerize Studio) or Enterprise-only (Everfit). Offering custom branding/white-label at the one-time per-trainer price attacks the highest-margin gate both hold.
5. **Speed + simplicity of a modern app.** "Clunky, dated, not smooth" (Trainerize) and "performance and speed" (Everfit) are recurring review themes. A fast, clean client app with AI programming included (not tiered) flanks both on experience while matching their AI checkbox.
